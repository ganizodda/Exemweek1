# INNOMA.VC Project

## 📌 Overview
INNOMA.VC is an international analytical and acceleration agency founded in 2021, headquartered in Bangkok, Thailand.  
It supports IT startups and technology businesses in expanding globally through **AI-driven analytics, research, and online accelerator programs**.  

This project is a simple **HTML-based presentation** about INNOMA.VC, designed to highlight its mission, services, and global impact.

---

## 🌍 Company Information
- **Founded:** 2021  
- **Headquarters:** Bangkok, Thailand  
- **Industry:** IT Services & Consulting, Startup Acceleration  
- **Company Size:** 11–50 employees  
- **Focus Regions:** Middle East, Asia, Latin America, Africa  

---

## 🚀 Services
- **Analytical Research:** AI-powered insights from 30+ global sources.  
- **Online Accelerator (8 weeks):** Training on IT trends, MVP development, unit economics, and investor readiness.  
- **AI Marketing Tools:** Workshops on branding, content planning, and lead generation.  
- **Global Partnering:** Connecting startups with investors, accelerators, and venture funds worldwide.
